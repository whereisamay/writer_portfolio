Payment Guides for your Store

By Amay Prabhu·5 min read

Step-by-step instructions to install and configure our payments plugin on your Shopify & WooCommerce store, so you can start accepting payments in just a few minutes.

Shopify

Before you begin, make sure you have an active Shopify store and a Finmo account with your Sandbox and Production API keys handy. You’ll find these in your Finmo Dashboard under Settings → Plugins → Shopify.

  1. Head to the Finmo Payments app on the Shopify App Store and click Install. This redirects you to your Shopify store.
  2. On your Shopify store, click the Install CTA. You’ll be redirected to the Finmo authentication page.
  3. Enter your Access Key and Secret Key for both the Sandbox and Production environments.
  4. Open the API Key details modal for each environment and make sure the status toggle is switched on. Both your Production and Sandbox keys need this enabled to work correctly.
  5. Submit your keys. You’ll be redirected back to your Shopify store to configure and activate the payment methods you want to offer customers at checkout.

Source: docs.finmo.net/docs/shopify

WooCommerce

Before you begin, make sure you have an active, verified Finmo account with the relevant payment methods (card, local methods, etc.) enabled.

  1. In your WordPress admin dashboard, go to Plugins → Add Plugin and search for “Finmo Payments”.
  2. Open the listing and click Install and activate.
  3. In Installed Plugins, locate Finmo Payments, click Manage, then tick the Enable Finmo Payments checkbox.
  4. In the Plugins module of your Finmo Dashboard, open the WooCommerce widget and copy the API Access Key and Secret Key. For testing, toggle Sandbox Mode on and copy the sandbox keys instead.
  5. Paste the keys into the plugin settings, and set the environment to match: Sandbox while testing, Production when you’re ready to go live.

Source: docs.finmo.net/docs/woocommerce

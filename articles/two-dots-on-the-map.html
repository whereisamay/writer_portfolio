Goa — that dot on the map

Growing up, I had always been in awe of Goa. Some call it the Vegas of India for the casinos & the parties, while others come to experience the slow paced country life & the serene beaches. Either way, Goa is always seen as a happy place where the mind randomly starts to play the “Maria Pitaache” song. Ever since I started travelling, I had always delayed exploring this place. It would appear like a small dot on the map, something which I thought would be a casual long weekend trip for another day. But boy was I so wrong!
